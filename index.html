When Snow Falls,
Movement Stops.

Exploring how heavy snowfall affects transportation, the challenges people face, and how thoughtful design can make winter travel safer and easier.

Explore the project ↓
01 · Understand

Problem Identification

Understanding the everyday transportation difficulties caused by snowfall.

Problem statement: Heavy snowfall can cover roads, reduce visibility, create slippery surfaces and delay public transport. This makes it difficult for students, workers, emergency services and local residents to travel safely and reach their destinations on time.
🚗

Blocked roads

Snow accumulation can make roads difficult or impossible to use.

🚌

Transport delays

Buses and other public transport may be delayed or cancelled.

🧊

Slippery surfaces

Ice increases the risk of vehicles skidding and pedestrians falling.

🌨️

Low visibility

Snowstorms can make it harder for drivers to see roads and signs.

03 · Feel the user's experience

Empathy Map

Considering what a person may think, feel, say and do during heavy snowfall.

💭 Thinks

“Will I reach college or work on time?”
“Is the road safe?”

❤️ Feels

Worried, cold, stressed and uncertain about travelling.

🗣️ Says

“The bus is late.”
“I need an updated road condition.”

👣 Does

Checks weather updates, waits for transport and searches for a safer route.

05 · Ideate

Possible Solutions

Design opportunities that could reduce winter travel difficulties.

📱

Live snow alerts

Provide timely weather, road closure and transport updates.

🧭

Safer route guidance

Help travellers identify open routes and avoid hazardous roads.

🚜

Snow clearance updates

Show which roads have been cleared and where work is underway.

🚌

Transport coordination

Share bus service changes and estimated arrival information.

07 · Conclusion

Making Winter Travel Safer

Snowfall is a natural challenge, but better information, safer routes and coordinated transport services can help people travel with greater confidence.

“A safer journey begins with understanding the traveller.”

Design thinking helps us understand real needs and develop human-centred solutions for everyday problems.
