DESIGN THINKING PROJECT

When Snow Falls,
Movement Stops.

Making winter transportation safer with weather updates, GPS location, snow alerts and travel directions.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Problem Identification

Understanding transportation difficulties caused by snowfall.

Problem statement: Heavy snowfall can cover roads, reduce visibility, create slippery surfaces and delay public transport. This makes it difficult for students, workers and local residents to travel safely and reach destinations on time.
🚗

Blocked Roads

Snow accumulation makes roads difficult to use.

🚌

Transport Delays

Buses and public transport may be delayed.

🧊

Slippery Roads

Ice increases the risk of accidents.

🌨️

Low Visibility

Snowstorms make it harder to see the road.
