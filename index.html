01 · UNDERSTAND

Problem Identification

Understanding transportation difficulties caused by heavy snowfall.

Problem Statement

Heavy snowfall can cover roads, reduce visibility, create slippery surfaces and delay public transport. This makes it difficult for students, workers and local residents to travel safely and reach their destinations on time.


🚗

Blocked Roads

Snow accumulation makes roads difficult to use.

🚌

Transport Delays

Buses and public transport may be delayed.

🧊

Slippery Roads

Ice increases the risk of accidents.

🌨️

Low Visibility

Snowstorms make driving difficult.

07 · CONCLUSION

Making Winter Travel Safer

Snowfall is a natural challenge, but better information, weather alerts and safer travel planning can help people travel with greater confidence.
